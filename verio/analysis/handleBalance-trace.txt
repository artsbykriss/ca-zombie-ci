# handleBalance() — decisive dynamic evidence (anvil fork of Story, block 23,623,495)

No mainnet transaction was signed. The WP proxy was called on a local anvil fork
(`anvil --fork-url https://mainnet.storyrpc.io --chain-id 1514`).

## 1. Destination of the surplus: WithdrawalPool InsurancePool, NOT the StakePool

Setup on fork: WP balance 0, Pw = 6,665.841927501986166900, E = 0,
insurance pool balance 263.583953655634299353, capacity 24,736.416046344366.

Donate 20,000 IP to WP, then call `WP.handleBalance()` as the StakePool
(`0xf670…1a27`). Result: WP balance = 6,665.841927501986166900 (exactly Pw),
StakePool balance unchanged, insurance pool +13,334.158072498014.

`debug_traceTransaction` (callTracer, abridged):


There is **no call to the StakePool with value** and **no path that raises
`address(this).balance` of the StakePool**. `fund()` (0xb60d4288) is called on the
insurance pool, not on 0xf670….

## 2. handleBalance() reserves excessUnstake E

Fork state reset; then `anvil_setStorageAt(SP, E_SLOT, 1000e18)` (E_SLOT =
`0xe41b…6709`, the 10th field of StakePoolState). Donate 10,000 IP to WP, call
`handleBalance()` as the StakePool. Value sent to insurance = **2,334.158072498014**
= 10,000 − Pw(6,665.841927501986166900) − E(1,000). WP retained Pw + E.
The trace reads `stakePool.getUnstaked()` (0x6c21157a) immediately before the
surplus computation, i.e. the reservation is explicit.

This means the pass-3 hypothesis ("surplus IP is returned to the StakePool and
E is still counted in totalStake") is **false**: E is held in the WP, not the SP,
and `address(this).balance` of the SP never sees it.

## 3. Where unbonded IP goes

`Delegator.setWithdrawalAddress()` (verified source) calls
`ipTokenStaking.setWithdrawalAddress{value: fee}(address(_selector().withdrawalPool()))`
— the precompile's withdrawal address is the **WithdrawalPool** (0x2a79…).
Unbonding settlement therefore mints IP to the WP, not to the StakePool.
`getTotalPendingWithdrawals()` is never read by the StakePool (grep of verified
StakePool source: only the interface declares it).

then `handleBalance()` as the StakePool:

- `getTotalStake()` delta: **0**
